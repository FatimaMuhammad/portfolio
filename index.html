About me

I got into mobile dev through a web bootcamp and never really looked back. Since then I've been working on Android and Flutter apps, mostly field and retail apps that people actually use every day, for clients in Pakistan and the GCC. Haazri

10K+ downloads, 4.4 rating

The problem

Attendance marked by hand is slow to collect and easy to fake. Managers end up reconciling paper sheets or self reported lists at the end of the day.

What I built

I worked on the attendance flow in Haazri, where check in and check out require a photo instead of just a tap. I developed the face verification piece so the person at the gate is the person on the roster.

Features

Facial detection for attendance verification built with ML Kit, liveness detection so a photo of a photo does not pass, and geofencing so check in only works from the right location. Records also work offline for sites with weak connectivity.

Tech stack

Kotlin, ML Kit, Retrofit, Coroutines, GitLab CI/CD.

My role

Feature development, enhancements, and release support on the app.

The result

The app is live on Google Play with 10K+ downloads and a 4.4 rating.
